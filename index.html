Our app · iOS & Android

dial

How others brew the same bag

A public brew repository for coffee people. Log the bags you buy and the cups you brew, then see how everyone else brewed the same coffee. We design, build and run all of it: the iOS and Android apps, the backend, and the share site.

Your cellar
Each bag with its roaster, roast date and weight. dial tracks what's left as you brew, and what's in the freezer.
Record your brews
Log the recipe, rate the cup. Brew it again and dial starts from your last recipe, so you change one thing at a time.
How others dialed it in
Every coffee has a page with everyone's brews of it. Start from a recipe that already worked.

Client app · iOS

EvenKeel

Custom insoles, ordered from a phone

EvenKeel makes performance insoles from a photo of your foot and a video of your stride. We build and maintain the iOS app people order them through, from the first photo to the delivery.

Guided capture
Foot photos and gait video with live level guides and instructions, so the footage is right the first time.
Material recommendation
Picks an insole material from each person's activities, pain points and profile.
Checkout & orders
Insoles for a whole household in one cart, Stripe payments, and order tracking through delivery.
