Our app · iOS & Android

dial

How others brew the same bag

A shared brew journal for specialty coffee. Log the bags you buy and the cups you brew. See how everyone else brewed the same coffee. We design, build and run all of it: the iOS and Android apps, the backend, and the site.

Your cellar
Each bag with its roaster, roast date and weight. dial tracks what's left as you brew, plus any coffee in the freezer.
Log your brews
Record the recipe, rate the cup. Brew it again and dial starts from your last recipe, so you change one thing at a time.
How others dialed it in
Every coffee has a page with everyone's brews of it. Start from a recipe that already worked.

Client app · iOS

EvenKeel

Custom insoles, ordered from your phone

EvenKeel makes performance insoles from a photo of your foot and a video of your stride. We completely rebuilt the iOS app people order them through, from first photo to delivery.

Guided capture
Foot photos and gait video with live level guides and instructions, so the footage is right the first time.
Material recommendation
Picks an insole material from each person's activities, pain points and profile.
Checkout & orders
Insoles for a whole household in one cart, Stripe payments, and order tracking through delivery.
